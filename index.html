About Us

Rusinga Trading Company is a fast-growing manufacturer and supplier of high-quality cleaning and personal care products, specializing in multipurpose liquid soap, toilet disinfectants, hand wash, bar soap, fabric softeners, and industrial degreasers etc. Our mission is to provide affordable, eco-friendly, and effective hygiene solutions for homes, businesses, and institutions.

With a strong commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction, we use carefully selected raw materials and adhere to industry standards to ensure that every product delivers excellent performance and safety.

Our Vision

To be a leading brand in the hygiene and sanitation industry in East Africa, known for quality, affordability, and environmental responsibility.

Our Mission

  • To produce superior-quality cleaning and personal care products.
  • To promote hygiene and health in communities.
  • To offer sustainable and eco-conscious manufacturing practices.
  • To create employment and empower local communities.

Core Values

Quality Assurance Customer Satisfaction Integrity Innovation Environmental Responsibility

Why Choose Us

Our Commitment to Quality and Safety

We take product quality seriously. We aim to use appropriate raw materials, follow controlled production procedures, maintain clean production and storage areas, and use suitable containers.

We also recognize the importance of proper labelling, safe handling instructions, batch identification, and applicable regulatory requirements.

We provide accurate information about our products and avoid making unsupported claims about their performance, safety, or suitability.

What Makes Us Different

We aim to stand out through five key commitments:

  • Value for money: Fair and competitive pricing.
  • Consistency: A commitment to maintaining product quality from batch to batch.
  • Convenience: Practical packaging options for regular users and bulk buyers.
  • Professionalism: Clear communication, honest product information, and transparent ordering arrangements.
  • Long-term partnerships: Building lasting relationships with retailers, distributors, and institutional customers.

Suitable for Different Customers

We serve different customer categories, including retailers, wholesalers, households, institutions, and commercial establishments.

We can discuss order quantities, product options, and supply arrangements depending on our production capacity and the customer's needs.
